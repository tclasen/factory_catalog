# Factory examples

[Catalog](../index.md) · [Ontology](../ontology.md) · [Adoption](../adoption.md)

- [Public research factory](research.md) — A public-source research workflow with claim review and explicit unknowns.
- [Workplace learning factory](learning.md) — A learning workflow that separates lesson delivery from improved ability.
- [Contract operations factory](contracts.md) — A contract workflow with distinct drafting, negotiation, signing, and records authority.

These are fictional designs that show how the ontology and controls compose. Their implementations are proposed and assessments have not been run. Their measures illustrate assessability; real adopters must justify measures for their context.
