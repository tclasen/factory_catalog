# Assess learning and transfer

[Adoption](../adoption.md) · [Learning example](../factories/learning.md)

## Purpose

Use when a factory aims to improve human ability. Apply [outcome verification](../controls/outcome-verification.md) and [verifier qualification](../controls/verifier-qualification.md) to a learning outcome. Observable competency and transfer inform this procedure.[^competency][^transfer] It is a proposed assessment design, not evidence that a lesson works.

## Design the assessment

1. State the capability, beneficiary, operating conditions, permitted assistance, rubric, threshold, observation period, and evaluator before delivery.
2. Use an entry task to identify prerequisite gaps. Absence of a demonstration is unknown; do not infer inability from missing evidence.
3. Choose practice for the observed gap, then an unseen related task to test transfer. Keep practice examples separate from acceptance tasks using [protected acceptance](../controls/protected-acceptance.md).
4. Record assistance and actual performance. Report recall, assisted completion, independent application, and missing follow-up separately. Compare baseline and follow-up under compatible conditions before claiming improvement.
5. Limit individual records to authorized readers and retention under [approved data processing](../controls/approved-data-processing.md). Put reusable content and rubrics in shared knowledge, with personal routes and observations in local records.

## Assessment fixtures and records

Compare repeating a worked example with explaining and completing a new scenario. Include a successful independent performance, an assisted performance, a completed lesson with a failed task, and absent follow-up. The first can satisfy its declared criterion; the others cannot silently satisfy an independent-transfer claim. If a rubric accepts a plausible wrong response or rejects a valid alternative, requalify it.

Retain target/rubric revisions, task identities, prerequisite observations, assistance, evaluator, time, results, and next actions. The reporting procedure passes when cases receive their declared dispositions and privacy restrictions hold; a false mastery claim or unauthorized record disclosure fails it. Missing observations are inconclusive. This does not establish that the teaching intervention caused improvement.
