# Proposal: handle conflicting source editions in research

**Status: unfinished proposal; not part of the qualified catalog bundle.** The
fixture below is proposed and has not been run. This note preserves a possible
research-example lesson while its procedure and assessment remain unfinished.

## Intended adopter decision

When a research memo compares product capabilities across sources or editions,
the researcher should identify which edition supports each claim, preserve
conflicting observations, and qualify a comparison while a material conflict is
unresolved. The distinct example would teach how evidence changes a comparative
claim, beyond the general source traceability and memo-coverage selections.

Proposed fixture: two editions disagree on one product capability. Expected
behavior is to identify the edition used, retain both observations with their
context, and qualify the comparison until the conflict is resolved. Silent edition
mixing or an unsupported definitive recommendation should fail. This fixture has
not been run; these expectations are not an assessment result or a qualified
procedure.
