# Open Group guides: opportunities for knowledge-work factories

Contributor research: use this material to develop controls and task-focused guides. It is outside the distributed OKF bundle; consult current control requirements before reuse.

[Ontology](../../catalog/ontology.md) · [Adoption](../../catalog/adoption.md) · [Work types](../work-types.md)

## Finding and evidence boundary

The most useful opportunities are connections between business purpose, organizational ability, stakeholder value, shared information, decision evidence, and cross-organization delivery. These can support policy, education, procurement, research, environmental reporting, and industrial planning as well as software work.

The research used the [Open Group Guides catalog](https://publications.opengroup.org/guides) and primary publisher pages, accessed **2026-09-29 UTC**. The catalog describes guides as supporting advice for adopting standards. A publication listing is evidence of a guide’s stated subject and edition; it is not evidence that a local implementation conforms or works.

### Coverage and method

- Scanned titles across all 20 pages of the main listing, which displayed 198 entries. These include translations, older editions, samples, and bundles, rather than 198 independent current methods.
- Read 33 selected public product descriptions and available metadata in detail, listed below. Selection favored reusable decisions and broad knowledge work; detailed coverage is purposive, not a systematic review of every guide’s contents.
- Cross-checked business architecture, Open FAIR, Open Footprint, and Open Process Automation category pages. The latter exposed G269, Version 2.0, which was absent from the main listing retrieved in this session. The 198-entry scan therefore does not establish the full current population.
- Checked the existing concept files and open contribution scopes to avoid turning generic software delivery, AI authority, or dependency-admission work into duplicate controls.
- Full PDF downloads displayed a login requirement. An attempted public HTML edition of Business Capabilities redirected to sign-in. **No full guide text was reviewed.** The rows below describe public abstracts and metadata; implementation requirements and test fixtures in the linked nodes are original catalog proposals motivated by those descriptions.
- No third-party copies, inferred chapter contents, standard clauses, certification criteria, or publisher performance claims are used as verified requirements. No `verified` event is asserted for these draft concepts.

Public-page metadata also needs scrutiny: the hospital guide’s “Common Name” field names Metadata Management, and the Roles and Skills page’s “Common Name” names Selecting Building Blocks. Use the publication code and displayed title to identify these sources; do not treat those inconsistent fields as ontology mappings.

## Prioritized node opportunities

### First: reuse across domains

The eight linked controls cover distinct decisions: whether to invest, whether a service stage is accepted, whether information has the same meaning, whether a risk estimate supports a choice, what supplier assurance covers, whether environmental totals reconcile, whether an exchange works across boundaries, and whose concerns a design addresses. They are independently selectable; using all eight is not a prerequisite for a factory.

The [capability, value, and information mapping guide](../../catalog/guides/capability-value-information-mapping.md) connects these decisions using existing concepts. It provides a compact record for abilities, activities, actors, artifacts, outcomes, and controls. Abilities and value stages are embedded descriptions, not new registered document types.

### Next: deepen domain coverage

| Opportunity | Beneficiary and work | Evidence needed before a dedicated node |
|---|---|---|
| Education service planning | Education authority planning enrollment, staffing, and learner support | Full SERM review, local capability definitions, affected-party review, and learner-outcome measures |
| Hospital administrative coordination | Administrative teams coordinating referrals, schedules, and information | Full ZiRA review, hospital workflow owners, access rules, and context-specific safety boundaries |
| Mentoring and professional development | Learners and staff developing demonstrable skills | Full G237/G249 review, role-specific performance rubric, observed practice, and feedback records |
| Resource-constrained transformation | Public leaders sequencing sustainable improvements | Full G221 review and local evidence on staff, access, operating cost, and institutional capacity |
| Quantitative risk composition | Decision owners choosing among mitigations | Full G180/G262/G263 methods, calibrated inputs, dependence analysis, and independently checked calculations |
| Environmental lifecycle decisions | Organizations comparing operating and embodied impacts | Full G242/G259 plus applicable accounting methods, boundaries, and domain-qualified assessment |
| Industrial modernization | Facility owners managing multi-supplier change | Full G269 and applicable standards, interface contracts, competent engineering and safety review |

These candidates are research directions, not established gaps in every adopter’s system. Select them from actual context. Maintain explicit unknowns until source and domain review justify stronger requirements.

### Retain as references or defer

Do not add a node for every publication, translation, pocket guide, or certification sample. Specialized UNIX, messaging, radio, FACE implementation, and sensor implementation guides become relevant when a factory actually depends on those technologies. Older guides can provide historical context, but implementation needs current applicable editions. The scan did not validate the normative contents or current certification status of any standard.

## Graph design and non-duplication

The additions use `Guide`, `Control`, and `Factory Example`, the existing [control families](../../catalog/control-families.md), and normal Markdown relationships. There is no new taxonomy, control family, schema, registry, or standard-as-authority edge.

- A factory **performs** activities that **produce** artifacts and **contribute to** an intended outcome. The mapping guide makes those connections explicit.
- A control **addresses** a described risk scenario or **supports** an outcome. Each new control states its failure mode and evidence procedure.
- An example’s `control_selections` records a proposal. It does not assert adoption, permission, implementation, or a passing assessment.
- `sources` records provenance. Citing an Open Group guide does not import that framework’s vocabulary or confer certification.

Existing [evidence traceability](../../catalog/controls/evidence-traceability.md) supports factual claims, [outcome verification](../../catalog/controls/outcome-verification.md) separates outputs from effects, and [bounded external action](../../catalog/controls/bounded-external-action.md) governs commitments. The additions specialize different decisions without weakening those requirements. Service-stage acceptance governs service value boundaries; [durable work handoff](../../catalog/controls/durable-work-handoff.md) preserves execution state. Supplier assurance scope evaluates procurement claims; dependency admission governs an execution environment. Stakeholder engagement identifies concerns; [decision rights and accountability](../../catalog/controls/decision-rights-and-accountability.md) assigns authority. [Accepted work definition](../../catalog/controls/accepted-work-definition.md) can consume the resulting concern-to-requirement record.

## Applying the findings

1. Describe the beneficiary outcome and actual operating context using [adoption](../../catalog/adoption.md).
2. Use the mapping guide to locate gaps in abilities, stage acceptance, shared meaning, or evidence.
3. Choose the relevant draft controls and record applicability and remaining gaps.
4. Obtain full guides and appropriate domain review before asserting any standard-specific mapping or requirement. Record exact editions and any differences from the public descriptions used here.
5. Implement the selected controls and execute their assessment cases with retained evidence. Draft instructions and repository validation are not operational assessments.
6. Pin the catalog revision consumed, and revisit sources when editions, stakeholders, boundaries, or decisions change.

The factory examples are fictional proposals: [public service design](../../catalog/factories/public-service-design.md), [environmental reporting](../../catalog/factories/environmental-reporting.md), and [industrial modernization procurement](../../catalog/factories/industrial-modernization-procurement.md). They illustrate knowledge work around broader operational domains; none claims deployment or effectiveness.
